What makes a report correct

  • The time is UTC, to the minute, and has gone by.
  • The frequency is a station's dial frequency, give or take half a kilohertz.
  • It quotes twelve characters in a row as they were sent, from that minute or the five either side of it. Where the station sent fewer than twelve, all of them. Spacing and capitals are not examined.

Nothing is kept, and nothing leaves this page. A report is written after the # in the address, which a browser does not send to anybody, and the page works out the answer for itself. Once a report is confirmed that address is the card, and the same report gets the same card again. The rules in full are section 16 of the Standard.
